OUR PURPOSE

About CleanMap

CleanMap is a research and development project exploring practical ways to reduce waste littering and improve public access to environmental information. The aim is to make littering locations, public dustbins, dumping grounds and cleanup history publicly accessible, while working constructively with communities and government authorities.

Disclaimer: CleanMap is not intended to troll, blame, target or accuse any person, community or government authority for waste littering or cleanup activities. Information is collected for research, public awareness and the development of better waste-management and processing solutions for the environment and public health. Community submissions are not official findings or proof of responsibility.

We welcome collaboration with government authorities, citizens, researchers and organisations.

PRODUCT ROADMAP

V1 and upcoming versions

V1 First testing version

  • Interactive map and community-created pins.
  • Dustbin, littering spot, drainage waste and dumping-ground categories.
  • Photo required to create a new pin.
  • Name, landmark, village / ward / area and map-derived address.
  • Public viewing of complete cleanup stages and timestamps.
  • No comments, public reviews or discussion section.
  • No government-recognised pins or government cleanup publishing in V1.

V1.1 Testing update

  • Single-photo site condition updates with newest records first.
  • Community-reported cleaned / not-cleaned status and counters.
  • Camera-capture request, browser limitations disclosed.
  • Cleanup workflow temporarily disabled.

Upcoming Future versions

  • Google sign-in and shared public submissions (requires Supabase configuration).
  • Central database, photo storage and moderation tools.
  • Government-verified dustbins and dumping locations after authority engagement.
  • Government cleanup activity records and transparency tools.
  • Navigation to nearby dustbins, toilets, littering zones and other mapped facilities.
  • Waste-processing, recycling and community solution features.

How to add a location

  1. Inspect the map first for an existing pin.
  2. Choose “Start placing a pin”, then click the exact map location.
  3. Drag the marker to adjust its position.
  4. Add the name, category, landmark and village / ward / local area.
  5. Attach a clear photo and save the pin.
  6. To document cleanup, submit before, mid-progress and after photos.

Records are saved only in this browser. Clearing browser data may remove them.
